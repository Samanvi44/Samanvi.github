Wonderland Theme Park, spread over 14 acres,is located about 15km from darjelling bus terminus 8km from the railway station on Nakodar Road. This park offers many thrilling rides. The water rides include bumper cars, flying jets, horror house, boating, kiddies' boating, play house, flying dragoans and a slide splash. Among the several atrractions the wave pool is probably the most popular. There is an aqua dance floor, where visitors can dance to the tune of the latest songs. The dance floor is underneath a canopy, which has artifital clouds from where the water comes down like a rainfall.The Day Park is open throughout the year,but the Water park is cloded during winter.
